Know the Most Common Violations

If you identify with the scenarios below, it is likely your rights are being disrespected by the company.

Fired and didn't get everything?

The employer withheld severance pay, invented excuses to delay, or failed to collect your retirement funds/fines properly.

Worked without a formal contract?

Provided daily services, received orders, but never had the formal link formalized? You're entitled to vacation time, bonuses, and benefits for all that period.

Did overtime without pay?

Working beyond the contractual hours, suppression of lunch breaks, and working on weekends require additional legal payment.

Suffered moral harassment or illness?

Endured humiliation, abusive demands, or developed depression and repetitive strain injuries due to a toxic work environment.
